This project is a tool for people who test charging infrastructure. Issues, pull requests and discussions are where they help each other, and they stay useful when everyone can take part.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with the code, the design or the specification reading, not with the person.
- Assume good faith, and say what you need: a reproduction, a clause number, a decision.
- Keep to the topic of the issue or pull request. Write in English so every maintainer and contributor can follow.
- Do not publish other people's private information, and do not post credentials, tokens or private CSMS addresses in traces and logs.

## What we do not accept

Harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or attention, threats, and repeated disruption after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies to the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

<!-- MAINTAINER: decide the contact. GitHub's "Report content" on a comment reaches the repository owner, but a private address or a GitHub private contact is better for reports about a person. Not invented here. -->

Report a problem to the maintainer, [@shiv3](https://github.com/shiv3), by a private route of their choice: _contact to be set by the maintainer_. Reports are handled privately where that is possible.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock a conversation, and block or ban someone from the repository. The usual order is a reminder, then a warning, then removal; a serious violation can skip steps.
